## Overview

StatusFlow AI is a WhatsApp Status planning and publishing assistant for vendors. The project is in development, with work focused on organizing reusable content and defining how it moves through approval and posting plans.

## Product direction

A vendor's content can include product listings, service descriptions, promotions, testimonials, and events. StatusFlow AI is being designed around a reusable library for these content types, so planning can start from an organized collection.

## What I am working on

- Designing the reusable library for products, services, promotions, testimonials, and events.
- Defining how content is reviewed and approved.
- Defining posting plans for WhatsApp Status.
- Developing the broader planning and publishing assistant.

## Current state

Publishing remains in development. The content library and approval flow described here are ongoing design work, and automated posting is not presented as a completed feature.
